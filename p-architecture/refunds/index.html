Fees and Cancellations

Last updated: October 18, 2026

We agree on fees before any work starts, so there are no surprises.

Before work starts

There is nothing to pay for sending an inquiry or for an initial chat about your project.

Once a proposal is signed

Payments, milestones and what happens if a project is paused or cancelled are written into the agreed proposal for your project.

If something isn't right
