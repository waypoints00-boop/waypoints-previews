How we work

From your idea to the final render.

  1. 01

    Book a consultation

    Pick a day and time that works for you, online or in person, and tell me a little about your space. Booking doesn't commit you to anything.

  2. 02

    Brief & scope

    We talk through your needs, lot or unit, and budget. You get a clear written scope, fee and timeline before any work starts.

  3. 03

    Design & visualization

    Layouts become photoreal 3D views, inside and out, so you can see the light, the materials and the feel of the space.

  4. 04

    Refine & hand over

    We adjust together until it feels right, then you receive the final renders and files agreed in your scope.
