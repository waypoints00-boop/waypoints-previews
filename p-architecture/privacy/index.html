Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Architect Patrick De Guzman, UAP (P.Architecture+) ("we") collects through this website, why, and how we protect it, in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its implementing rules.

Who we are

Architect Patrick De Guzman, UAP (P.Architecture+) · architectpatrickd@gmail.com.

For any privacy question or request, contact us at architectpatrickd@gmail.com.

What we collect

Only what you type into our forms:

  • your name, email address and (if you add it) mobile number;
  • if you book a consultation: the day, time and type of meeting (online or in person) you pick;
  • details about your project: the type of project, its city or location, approximate size, what stage it is in, your timeline and the message you write.

We do not ask for passwords, OTPs, PINs or card numbers.

Why we use it

  • to schedule and hold the consultation you booked, and send you its details;
  • to reply to your inquiry and talk about your project;
  • to prepare a proposal or quote if you ask for one;
  • to keep a basic record of inquiries we receive.

We use your information only for these purposes. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising.

Our legal basis

We process your information with your consent (given when you tick the consent box on our forms) and because we need it to respond to your request or to provide what you booked or ordered. We may also keep records when the law requires it, such as for tax purposes.

Who we share it with

We share your information only with the service providers that run this website and our records, and only as needed:

ProviderWhat they do for us
Netlifyhosts this website
Google (Apps Script, Sheets, Calendar, Gmail)stores bookings and inquiries in our private sheet, adds booked consultations to our private calendar, and sends confirmation emails

Some of these providers store data on servers outside the Philippines. They are required to keep it secure. We may also disclose information if the law or a lawful order requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep bookings and inquiries for as long as we need them to talk with you about your project, and up to 2 years after our last message if no project starts. Project records are kept as long as the law requires. You can ask us to delete your details anytime, except records we must keep by law.

How we protect it

  • Our website is served over HTTPS (encrypted).
  • Form entries go to a private Google Sheet that only authorized people can open.
  • Our website runs no ads, tracking or analytics.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your data is used;
  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have incorrect data corrected;
  • object to processing and withdraw your consent;
  • have your data deleted or blocked when it is no longer needed or was processed unlawfully;
  • get a copy of your data (data portability);
  • be compensated for damages caused by a data breach; and
  • file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

To use any of these rights, contact us at architectpatrickd@gmail.com. We'll respond as soon as we can.

Children

This website is meant for adults. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us for you.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
