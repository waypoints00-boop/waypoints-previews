Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Short version: this website does not use cookies for tracking, advertising or analytics, so there is no cookie banner to click.

What we use instead

To make the site work, your own browser may remember a few small things on your device (using "local storage"). This information stays on your device; we never receive it.

  • whether you've already seen the opening animation (cleared when you close the tab);
  • while a booking is waiting for your down payment: its booking number and a private key, so you can come back to the QR and upload your receipt (removed once your booking is confirmed, and after 3 days at most).

You can clear it anytime by clearing your browser's site data.

Third parties

This website does not load ads, social media trackers or analytics. Its fonts and scripts are hosted on our own website. Our hosting provider may record basic technical information (such as your IP address and browser type) in its server logs for security.

Links to other sites (Facebook Messenger, Google Maps) open those sites, which have their own cookie and privacy policies.

If this changes

If we ever add analytics or other non-essential cookies, we'll update this page and ask for your consent first.
