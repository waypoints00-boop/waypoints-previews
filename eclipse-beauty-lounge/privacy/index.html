Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Eclipse Beauty Lounge ("we") collects through this website, why, and how we protect it, in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its implementing rules.

Who we are

Eclipse Beauty Lounge · 2nd Floor, 117 Gen. Ordoñez St., Marikina Heights, Marikina City · Facebook Messenger (m.me/61593057140350).

For any privacy question or request, contact us through Facebook Messenger (m.me/61593057140350).

What we collect

Only what you type into our forms:

  • your name, mobile number and email address;
  • the service, day and time you want;
  • your Facebook name and notes, if you add them;
  • the screenshot of your down payment receipt and the name on the account you paid from. A receipt usually shows the amount, date, reference number, and the sender and receiver names or masked numbers.

We do not ask for passwords, OTPs, PINs or card numbers.

Why we use it

  • to hold your slot and confirm your appointment;
  • to check your down payment: our booking system reads the text on your receipt and compares the amount, names and reference number with your booking, and we may check it by hand;
  • to stop the same receipt from being used twice;
  • to contact you about your booking by email, text, call or Messenger;
  • to keep basic records of bookings and payments.

We use your information only for these purposes. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising.

Our legal basis

We process your information with your consent (given when you tick the consent box on our forms) and because we need it to respond to your request or to provide what you booked or ordered. We may also keep records when the law requires it, such as for tax purposes.

Who we share it with

We share your information only with the service providers that run this website and our records, and only as needed:

ProviderWhat they do for us
Netlifyhosts this website
Google (Apps Script, Sheets, Drive, Gmail)stores bookings in our private sheet, keeps receipt screenshots in our private Drive folder, reads the text on receipts, and sends booking emails

Some of these providers store data on servers outside the Philippines. They are required to keep it secure. We may also disclose information if the law or a lawful order requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep booking records for as long as we need them to serve you and as long as the law requires for payment and tax records. Receipt screenshots are deleted 12 months after your appointment. You can ask us to delete your details anytime, except records we must keep by law.

How we protect it

  • Our website is served over HTTPS (encrypted).
  • Form entries go to a private Google Sheet that only authorized people can open.
  • Our website runs no ads, tracking or analytics.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your data is used;
  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have incorrect data corrected;
  • object to processing and withdraw your consent;
  • have your data deleted or blocked when it is no longer needed or was processed unlawfully;
  • get a copy of your data (data portability);
  • be compensated for damages caused by a data breach; and
  • file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

To use any of these rights, contact us through Facebook Messenger (m.me/61593057140350). We'll respond as soon as we can.

Children

This website is meant for adults. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us for you.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
