Refund Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Freshly baked, made to order. Here's what happens if plans change or something isn't right.

Cancelling an order

  • Cancel at least 3 days before your date needed for a full refund.
  • Personalized items (printed cards, stickers, custom packaging) can't be refunded once we've started making them.

If something isn't right

Message us within 24 hours of receiving your order, with photos, and we'll replace the item or refund it.

How refunds are sent

Approved refunds go back to the same payment method, usually within 7 business days.
