Do I pay anything every month?

Not to us. It's a one-time price. If you want your own domain (like yourbusiness.com), that's about ₱700–1,000 a year, paid to the domain provider. There are no payment company fees: money goes straight to your own GCash, Maya or bank.

Which version should I choose?

If customers already pay you by GCash, cash or bank and you just want bookings or orders organized, choose ₱7,000. If you want customers to pay right on your website and get automatic receipts, choose ₱14,000.

What do I need for online payments?

Just your own GCash, Maya or bank QR. Payments go straight to you; we never touch your money.

How do I know the QR on this page is really yours?

It only appears after you enter the reference and email from your mock-up email, and the account name is shown under the QR. Check that name in your app before you pay. If anything looks different, email us first at way.points00@gmail.com.

Can I still change things on my design?

Yes. Tell us in the form, or reply to any of our emails. We finish your changes before launch.

Who owns the website?

You do. It's your business, your brand and your website.

What if I'm not ready yet?
