Why we do this

A customer messages you at 11 PM to ask for your price. By the time you reply in the morning, they have booked someone else. We build websites that *answer* *the* *price* *question,* *take* *the* *booking* and *send* *the* *confirmation* while you sleep.

What runs behind it

The tools we build with,
and who owns them.

Your orders, leads and emails live in your own Google account, and your website files and domain are yours to keep. Here is what each part runs on.

Your data

Your own Google Sheet

Orders, bookings and leads land in a sheet in your Google account. Open it, filter it or download it whenever you like.

The engine

Google Apps Script

Runs inside your Google account and does the saving, emailing, slot checks and reminders. The basics need no extra app subscription.

Emails

Sent from your own Gmail

Confirmations and follow-ups go out from your address, so customer replies come back to you.

Payments

The payment method you choose

Stripe, PayPal, Square, GCash, a bank QR or a plugin you already use. The money goes straight to your account.

Hosting

Fast hosting, files you keep

We host on Netlify and keep your site files in a Git repository. The website and the domain are yours.

Before launch

Tested before we hand it over

We try to break every booking and payment flow first, with fake receipts and edited payment links, and you get written setup steps.
