Booking & Refund Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

We want booking with us to feel easy and fair. Here's what happens if your plans change.

Down payment

  • The down payment is deducted from your bill on the day.
  • Cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before your appointment and we'll refund your down payment in full or move it to your new date.
  • Cancellations within 24 hours or no-shows forfeit the down payment.
  • If we have to cancel, you get a full refund.
  • If you paid but your receipt couldn't be confirmed and the slot was released, we refund you in full.

If you're not happy

Tell us as soon as possible so we can make it right.

How refunds are sent

Approved refunds are sent back to the same GCash, Maya, card or bank account you paid from, usually within 7 business days. Refunds of online payments go back through PayMongo; card refunds can take longer to show, depending on your bank.
