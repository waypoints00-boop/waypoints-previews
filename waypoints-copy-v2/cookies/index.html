Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Short version: we use Google Analytics to understand how people use this website, but only if you say yes in our cookie banner. Saying no is just as easy, and the site works the same either way.

Analytics cookies (only with your OK)

If you tap Accept, Google Analytics sets these cookies on your device:

CookiePurposeKept for
_gaTells one visitor apart from another, without your name2 years
_ga_<ID>Remembers your current visit2 years

They show us which pages people view, which buttons they tap (for example "Book a free mock-up"), roughly where visitors are from (city or country) and what device they use. Google Analytics doesn't store IP addresses. We turned off Google's advertising features, and we never send your name, email or form answers to Google. Google may process this data on servers outside the Philippines; see Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choice

Nothing from Google loads until you tap Accept. If you tap Reject, no analytics cookies are set. We save your choice and its date on your device (local storage, key "wp-consent") so we don't keep asking, and we ask again after 12 months. To change your mind anytime, tap Cookie settings at the bottom of any page. If you switch to Reject, we delete the Google Analytics cookies from this site.

Other things stored on your device

To make the site work, your browser may also remember:

  • Only if you open our portfolio sample (pages under /work/): whether you've already seen the sample's opening animation.

This stays on your device; we never receive it.

Other third parties

This website does not load ads or social media trackers. Its fonts and scripts are hosted on our own website. Our hosting provider may record basic technical information (such as your IP address and browser type) in its server logs for security.
