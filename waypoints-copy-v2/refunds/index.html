Refund Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

You see your full design for free before you pay anything, so you only pay once you're happy with it. Because of that, payments aren't refundable if you simply change your mind. Your rights under Philippine consumer law still apply, as explained below.

Change of mind

Once you've paid to launch, we don't refund the payment if you change your mind. Please take your time with the free mock-up and ask for any changes before you pay.

If we can't deliver

If we can't put your website live within 14 days of your payment because of a problem on our side, we refund you in full.

If something doesn't work as agreed

If something we built doesn't work the way we agreed, tell us and we'll fix it at no cost. If we can't fix it, we'll refund you for the part that doesn't work.

How refunds are sent

Refunds go back through the same payment method within 7 business days of approval. Fees charged by other companies (domain registrars, payment providers) can't be refunded by us. Questions? Email way.points00@gmail.com.
