Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Pop Haus Staycation ("we") collects through this website, why, and how we protect it, in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its implementing rules.

Who we are

Pop Haus Staycation · Shell Residences Tower B, Mall of Asia Complex, Pasay City · pophaus.staycation@gmail.com · 0976 520 0921.

For any privacy question or request, contact us at pophaus.staycation@gmail.com.

What we collect

Only what you type into our forms:

  • your name, mobile number and email;
  • the unit, dates and number of guests you choose;
  • notes you write (for example, a late arrival or a celebration);
  • before check-in: the valid ID details the building needs to register guests;
  • if you pay online: your payment confirmation from PayMongo (we never see your card or e-wallet details).

We do not ask for passwords, OTPs, PINs or card numbers. Online payments are made on PayMongo's secure checkout page, so we never see or store your card or e-wallet details.

Why we use it

  • to confirm and manage your booking;
  • to send your check-in instructions and contact you about your stay;
  • to register you with the building administration, as Shell Residences requires for every guest;
  • to send your payment confirmation and return your security deposit;
  • to keep basic records of bookings and payments.

We use your information only for these purposes. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising.

Our legal basis

We process your information with your consent (given when you tick the consent box on our forms) and because we need it to respond to your request or to provide what you booked or ordered. We may also keep records when the law requires it, such as for tax purposes.

Who we share it with

We share your information only with the service providers that run this website and our records, and only as needed:

ProviderWhat they do for us
Netlifyhosts this website and may keep a backup copy of booking requests
Google (Apps Script, Sheets, Gmail)stores bookings in our private sheet and sends booking emails
PayMongoprocesses online payments
Shell Residences building administrationreceives guest names and ID details for building security and registration

Some of these providers store data on servers outside the Philippines. They are required to keep it secure. We may also disclose information if the law or a lawful order requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep booking records for as long as we need them to serve you and as long as the law requires for payment and tax records. ID details shared for building registration are deleted from our records after your stay. You can ask us to delete your details anytime.

How we protect it

  • Our website is served over HTTPS (encrypted).
  • Form entries go to a private Google Sheet that only authorized people can open.
  • Our website runs no ads, tracking or analytics.
  • Payments are processed by PayMongo, a payment provider registered with the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your data is used;
  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have incorrect data corrected;
  • object to processing and withdraw your consent;
  • have your data deleted or blocked when it is no longer needed or was processed unlawfully;
  • get a copy of your data (data portability);
  • be compensated for damages caused by a data breach; and
  • file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

To use any of these rights, contact us at pophaus.staycation@gmail.com. We'll respond as soon as we can.

Children

This website is meant for adults. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us for you.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
