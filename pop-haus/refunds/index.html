Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Plans change. Here's what happens to your payment if you need to cancel or move your stay.

Moving your dates

Message us at least 7 days before check-in and we'll move your booking to new available dates once, at no cost. Rate differences apply.

Cancelling

  • Cancel at least 7 days before check-in: we refund your payment minus the reservation amount (50% of one weeknight).
  • Cancel less than 7 days before check-in, or no-show: your payment isn't refundable.
  • If we have to cancel, you get a full refund.

Security deposit

Your ₱1,000 security deposit is returned within 24 hours after check-out, minus any charges for damage or missing items, which we'll explain with photos.

How refunds are sent

Approved refunds go back to the same payment method through PayMongo, usually within 7 business days.
