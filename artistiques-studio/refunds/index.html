Booking Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

We only take a few clients a day, so we hold your slot just for you. Here's what happens if your plans change.

Down payment

  • The down payment is 50% of your agreed price and is deducted from your total on the day.
  • It is non-refundable if you cancel, arrive more than 15 minutes late, or don't show up.
  • If we have to cancel or can't do your appointment, you get a full refund.
  • If you paid but your receipt couldn't be confirmed and the slot was released, we refund you in full.

Rescheduling

  • Message us 2–5 days before your appointment to move it and keep your down payment.
  • Rescheduling 1 day before or on the day of your appointment needs a new down payment.
  • Send your new preferred date and time, and we'll help you find a slot.

If you're not happy

Tell us as soon as possible so we can make it right. Your rights under the Consumer Act of the Philippines still apply.

How refunds are sent

Approved refunds are sent to the same GCash or bank account you paid from, usually within 7 business days.
